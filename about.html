About Saloyal Cargo Solution

Built to move things — and be trusted while doing it

We are a leading transport and logistics company providing reliable, efficient and customer-focused solutions across Kenya and beyond.

Our Story

Logistics, coordinated with intention

Saloyal Cargo Solution was founded on a simple idea: transport doesn't have to be a source of stress for the businesses that depend on it. From container haulage at the port to the last mile in a rural county, we plan every leg of the route so our clients don't have to think twice about where their goods are.

Based in Nairobi, our fleet and coordination team work across Kenya's counties, connecting importers, distributors and manufacturers with dependable transport partners.

Vision

To become one of Kenya's most trusted logistics and transport service providers by connecting businesses with efficient and dependable solutions.

Mission

To deliver on quality, efficiency, and affordability while building long-term partnerships with our clients and transport partners.
